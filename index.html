🍽️ Fuel

🍽️ Portion guide — your hand is the measure

No weighing, no app-within-an-app. Your hand scales with you.

Palm — protein (chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt)
Fist — vegetables and fruit
Cupped hand — carbs (rice, potatoes, bread, oats)
Thumb — fats (butter, oil, peanut butter, nuts)

One simple rule carries the rest: protein first at every meal, then everything else. There's no food database and no meal log here on purpose — just the check on today's check-in.

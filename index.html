🧰 Coping ideas

  • Step outside for 5 minutes of fresh air.
  • Drink a big glass of cold water.
  • Make a cup of tea or coffee, slowly.
  • Text or call someone you trust.
  • Take a shower — reset your body.
  • Write down exactly what you're feeling right now.
  • Go for a short walk around the block.
  • Head to a meeting — there's one every day at 5:30 PM.

🎬 Play the tape forward

Pause and picture it honestly: the first drink, then the next ones, then tomorrow morning — how you feel waking up, what it costs you, who it affects. Now picture the other version: you ride this out, and wake up tomorrow clear, with your streak intact. Which morning do you want?
